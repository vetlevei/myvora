Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

1. About Myvora

Myvora is a personal iOS application for training, performance, sleep and recovery. Myvora is operated by Vetle Veierød in Norway.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Myvora application or visit this website.

2. Information Myvora may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, Myvora may process:

  • Sleep and physiological data that you authorize through Oura.
  • Sleep duration, sleep stages, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature deviation, movement, sleep efficiency and blood-oxygen information when available.
  • Training sessions, heart-rate data and workout summaries.
  • Wellness information that you enter yourself.
  • Profile preferences, app settings and connected-sensor settings.
  • OAuth authorization credentials required to maintain an authorized connection.

Myvora only requests information needed for features that you choose to enable.

3. How the information is used

The information is used to:

  • Display your sleep, recovery and training information.
  • Calculate explainable Sleep Recovery and Personal Recovery Status results.
  • Provide trends, reports and training guidance.
  • Maintain connections to services and sensors you authorize.
  • Improve reliability and correct technical errors.

Myvora's recovery calculations use defined, deterministic rules. Oura's own scores may be shown for comparison but are not used as hidden inputs in Myvora's recovery calculations.

4. Oura connection and consent

Connecting Oura is optional. Myvora will only access Oura information after you explicitly authorize the connection through Oura's consent process. You may revoke that authorization through Myvora or through your Oura account.

Oura data is provided by Oura and remains subject to Oura's own terms and privacy practices. Myvora is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Oura.

5. Storage and security

During the initial personal testing phase, Oura-derived information is intended to be stored locally on the user's iPhone. OAuth credentials are intended to be stored using Apple's Keychain security system. Myvora does not currently operate a cloud database for personal Oura data.

If cloud synchronization or a server component is introduced later, this policy will be updated before that processing begins.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information. However, no digital storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Artificial intelligence

Oura data obtained through the Oura API is not sent to ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot or other general-purpose artificial-intelligence or large-language-model services. It is not used to train, fine-tune, evaluate or prompt an AI model.

Generic development assistance may use synthetic test data that does not contain personal or Oura-derived information.

7. Sharing and sale of information

Myvora does not sell personal or health information and does not use it for advertising. Personal Oura data is not published in this public GitHub repository or on this website.

Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect legal rights and security.

8. Legal basis

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, health and physiological information is processed on the basis of your explicit consent. Necessary app settings and technical information may be processed to provide the service you request and to maintain its security and reliability.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by disconnecting the relevant service. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

9. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed to provide the selected features or meet legal requirements.

You may request deletion of information associated with Myvora. Where Myvora stores information that is subject to an Oura deletion request, it will be deleted as soon as reasonably possible and within 72 hours where required by the applicable Oura agreement.

Locally stored app information may also be removed by using available deletion controls or deleting the application from the device.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or information about the processing of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority.

11. This website

This public website does not collect health information and does not use advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and security logs in accordance with GitHub's own privacy practices.

12. Children

Myvora is not directed at children. Connections to third-party health services are subject to the age requirements of those services and applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Myvora's features or data processing change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: myvora.app@hotmail.com
