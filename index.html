Myvora

Know Your Body. Guide Your Training.

Myvora is a personal iOS application in development for training, performance and recovery. It combines user-authorized physiological, sleep and training data to provide transparent and explainable insights that support daily training decisions.

Your data. Your decisions.

Myvora is designed around data minimization, explicit user consent and transparent calculations. Health data is never used to train artificial intelligence models.
